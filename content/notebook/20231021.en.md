# 20231021 Lab Visit: Kurita Lab

I visited the [Kurita Lab](https://soft.fpark.tmu.ac.jp/) with [Mr. Shimamoto](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.22369) and many others. During a break at [Kanto Soft Matter](https://soft.fpark.tmu.ac.jp/ks/ks2023.html), a conversation with Mr. Shimamoto and a member of the Kurita Lab led to an invitation to tour the lab. As we gathered more people interested in joining, the group became unexpectedly large.
